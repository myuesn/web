A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you get in touch.

Why are there no prices online?＋

B2B quotations depend on the exact part, quantity, availability and export requirements. Send an enquiry for terms specific to your request.

How do I confirm that a part will fit?＋

Include the OEM reference, machine make, model and year. The sample catalogue is a starting point, not a compatibility guarantee.

Can I send multiple parts in one enquiry?＋

Yes. Add parts to your enquiry list, adjust quantities and include any additional references in the form.

Is the enquiry sent automatically?＋

No. The form prepares a draft in your email app. Review and send it there, or download the text file and email it yourself.
